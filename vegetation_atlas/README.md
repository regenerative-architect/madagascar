# Madagascar Living Vegetation Atlas — v1.0

**Produced:** 27 October 2026  
**Credits:** Foster + Navi / Planetary Restoration Archive  
**Plant and habitat profiles:** 35 | **Ecological groupings:** 9 | **Reviewed source links:** 35

## Open

Open `index.html` directly in a modern browser. It is a complete standalone HTML page; CSS, scripts and catalog are embedded. No server, build step, account or API is needed.

`species_catalog.json` is a machine-readable export of the same catalog. `source_manifest.json` records institutional and scientific references.

## Safety first

Not every plant that survives drought is safe to introduce. Local native vegetation restoration is separate from already-established cultivated crops on existing farmland. The atlas does **not** authorize novel plant imports, wild collection, movement of protected/endemic species, or replacement of native grasslands and wetlands with trees. Site-specific review by Malagasy communities, agronomists and botanists is essential.

## Language

The primary guide and all plant profiles are in English, with an explicitly labeled partial French field summary. A validated Malagasy-language translation remains future work; no incomplete translation is presented as complete.

## Functional modules

- Static, print-friendly ecosystem and species cards accessible without JavaScript.
- Native/farm/survey-first filters and responsive search.
- District planner with illustrative density and roof rainwater-capture estimates.
- IndexedDB observations and shortlist; localStorage/memory fallback as availability permits.
- JSON backup (schema v1), validated JSON restore, CSV observation export and confirmed reset.
- Light/dark UI, collapsible sidebar and native details accordions, keyboard support and reduced-motion styles.

## Constraints

- No offline copies of external research pages or seed inventories. Supplier links require internet.
- No live weather, stock, prices, map or legal status feed is bundled.
- Individual species cards are planning candidates or habitat concepts, **not** parcel-level approval.
- Browser data can be cleared and does not synchronize. Export regularly.
- Water collection arithmetic does not determine safe drinking water, pump yield, or groundwater sustainability.
